# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project — filing issues, opening PRs, asking questions — to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what's best for the project and its users

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and to any individual representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the repository owner ([@khuisman](https://github.com/khuisman)) via a GitHub direct message or by opening a private report through the repository's [Security tab](../../security). All complaints will be reviewed and result in a response deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances, at the maintainer's discretion — this is a small, solo-maintained project without a dedicated moderation team.
