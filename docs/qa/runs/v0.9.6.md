## Process notes

- **Playwright sharing (Run 2).** Seven browser shards ran on four Playwright servers, two per server under a lock whose 180 s staleness was shorter than a capture batch. Locks were broken, captures crossed (all caught by a Doc-ID check and retaken), servers hung for 30 minutes at a time and then disconnected. The retakes ran one shard per server with no incident. To change in `aziz.md`/`run.md`: no more browser shards than `playwright-<k>` servers, no shared-server fallback, and ask for more servers before planning more.
- **Tab cleanup.** `run.md` forbids closing tabs, so 16 accumulated. Run 3's shards closed their own tab as their last step; Aziz reset the browser to one tab after Run 2.
- **Sub-agent spending.** Shard prompts didn't forbid starting agents; one shard started nine. Run 3's prompts forbid it.
- **Disk-full stop.** Resumed shards lost their context (transcripts couldn't be written); their reported results were transcribed and the rest re-spawned.
- **Unicode through the tool channel.** Combining marks, U+00A0, U+2009 and U+212A are normalized before a tool call; cases that depend on them need a script that reads the bytes.
- **Gmail fixtures.** Gmail deleted the spam/trash fixtures after 30 days (#1420).
- Stale case wording found by shards is listed at the top of each shard's results; none changed a verdict.
