# Lisbon Trip — November 6–10

## Flights

| | Flight | Departs | Arrives |
|---|---|---|---|
| Out | TP 218 | Nov 6, 18:40 (EWR) | Nov 7, 06:55 (LIS) |
| Back | TP 217 | Nov 10, 11:15 (LIS) | Nov 10, 14:30 (EWR) |

**Hotel:** Casa do Bairro, Rua da Rosa 42 — check-in from 15:00

## Day by day

### Thursday, Nov 7

1. Land, drop bags at the hotel
2. Late breakfast at a *pastelaria* near Praça do Comércio
3. Afternoon: walk Alfama
   - Miradouro de Santa Luzia
   - Sé Cathedral
4. Dinner: Taberna da Rua das Flores (no reservations; arrive by 19:00)

### Friday, Nov 8

1. Day trip to Sintra
   - Train from Rossio, every 20 min, ~40 min ride
   - **Pena Palace**: timed tickets, booked for 10:30
   - Quinta da Regaleira in the afternoon
2. Back in Lisbon by 18:00

### Saturday, Nov 9

- Morning: LX Factory and the Sunday market (*it runs Saturdays too*)
- Afternoon: Belém
  - Jerónimos Monastery
  - Pastéis de Belém (expect a line)
- Evening: fado show, 21:00

### Sunday, Nov 10

- Pack, check out by 09:00, taxi to the airport
