### ES-01 — backslash escapes for every ASCII punctuation character

\! \" \# \$ \% \& \' \( \) \* \+ \, \- \. \/ \: \; \< \= \> \? \@ \[ \\ \] \^ \_ \` \{ \| \} \~

### ES-02 — escaped dollar amounts (#213)

The plan costs \$12 per seat, or \$120 per year.

### ES-03 — escaped emphasis markers

\*not italic\* and \*\*not bold\*\* and \_not italic\_.

### ES-04 — escaped list and heading markers at line start

\- not a bullet

1\. not a numbered item

\# not a heading

### ES-05 — HTML entities

&amp; &lt; &gt; &quot; &copy; &mdash; &nbsp;(nbsp) &#169; &#x2603;

### ES-06 — raw angle brackets and ampersands

1 < 2 and 3 > 2 and Tom & Jerry and AT&T.

### ES-07 — backslash before a non-punctuation character stays

C:\Users\name\file.txt and a\b.
