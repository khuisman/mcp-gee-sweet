### EM-01 — italic with star and underscore

Star *italic* and underscore _italic_.

### EM-02 — bold with stars and underscores

Star **bold** and underscore __bold__.

### EM-03 — bold italic

Triple ***bold italic*** and mixed **_bold italic_** and _**italic bold**_.

### EM-04 — nested emphasis

**Bold with *italic inside* and back to bold.**

*Italic with **bold inside** and back to italic.*

### EM-05 — intraword

Star intra**word**bold and intra*word*italic.

Underscore snake_case_name stays plain (no italics).

### EM-06 — strikethrough

### EM-07 — emphasis markers with spaces are literal

A * star with spaces * stays literal, and so does 2 * 3 * 4.
